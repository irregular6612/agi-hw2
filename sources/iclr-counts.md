# iclr-counts — ICLR submissions that mention "looped", 2024–2027

**Citation (slide footer):** OpenReview search API, term "looped", ICLR.cc/{2024,2025,2026,2027}/Conference, forum notes; retrieved 21 Nov 2026.

**Method.** `https://api2.openreview.net/notes/search?term=looped&group=ICLR.cc/<year>/Conference&source=forum&limit=1000`, then a check that the word "looped" appears in the title, abstract or keywords (every returned note passed). All submissions are counted, including withdrawn and desk-rejected ones.

\* "looped" followed within three words by transformer, model, LM/LLM, language, network, architecture, reasoning, block, layer, depth, computation, attention or recurrence. The rest are mostly other senses (e.g. "open-looped" control, "looped task execution") or looped variants of other methods (e.g. "looped mixture-of-experts").

ICLR 2027 status breakdown (21 Nov 2026): 171 under review, 9 desk-rejected, 9 withdrawn.

**Caveats.**
- These are search-index counts, not a full scan. A full listing of all ICLR submissions was blocked by OpenReview's bot challenge, so completeness for older years could not be cross-checked.
- Multi-word queries ("looped transformer", "recurrent depth") hit the 1,000 cap and behave like OR searches, so only the single word is used.
- A submission is not a validated result. Use the numbers as a sign of attention only.
- Do not claim the Astra report caused the 2027 jump; we only know the two coincide in time.
